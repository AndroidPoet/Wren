Third-party frames get no storage when third-party cookies are blocked or Tor is on: a tracker's frame on one site could write localStorage, IndexedDB, caches or files and read them back from its frame on another site, joining the two visits as a cookie would; now every such API answers with the SecurityError Chrome gives it.
Tor: Web Workers now run the same shims as the page (UTC clock on the 100 ms grid, blank canvas, four cores, en-US); every locale-sensitive API answers en-US unless the page names a locale; pointer and touch events report screen coordinates equal to client coordinates; keys the US layout cannot type report an empty code; shims read as native code through Function.prototype.toString.
Tor: a page's alert, confirm and prompt dialogs are hidden from screenshots and recents like the page; the settings, library and downloads screens too while Tor is on; Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control are not sent under Tor (Tor Browser sends neither); a pooled clear-net connection reused under Tor is cancelled, not just reported.
Tor: a service worker of the clear-net profile is cut off when Tor comes on, a private one when the route changes; a popup's transport joins the opener's profile; switching to a saved tab waits for the route; a failed circuit renewal no longer leaves the proxy pointing at the old port.
Downloads with cookies go through the in-app downloader, which scopes a hop's cookies to its origin and drops Secure cookies on http hops (the hop cookie header itself was malformed before); Tor on stops every system download the manager still lists; a download paused before it started stays paused.
Fingerprinting: WebGL2 buffer readbacks, decoded video frames and the byte-valued audio analyser reads carry the same per-site noise as the canvas, and are blank under Tor.
