Security and privacy round: the ad blocker no longer refuses same-site fetch and XMLHttpRequest calls that have no file extension (a mis-parsed list rule did, on every site, since 1.0.4); internet pages cannot reach the phone's own network; link ping trackers are removed as a link is tapped; certificate error pages say what was wrong; mixed content is never loaded; a Safe Browsing switch in Settings; WebGPU removed in private tabs; private tabs without engine profile support get no network instead of the normal cookies; HTTPS-only now covers long-press downloads.
