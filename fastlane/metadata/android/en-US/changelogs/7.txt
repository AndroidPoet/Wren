Tor: bridges (obfs4, Snowflake, meek, or your own lines) for networks that block Tor; a failed Tor start now points every tab at a closed port instead of the open network; new identity drops the open pages before the circuits change.
Tor fix: downloads, site icons and filter-list updates could leave outside Tor, because Android's built-in HTTP client falls back to a direct connection when a proxy fails and the proxy address was the IPv6 loopback, where Tor does not listen. They now use a client that refuses any connection under Tor that is not the tunnel.
Downloads: a download started under Tor only ever resumes or retries through Tor; redirects are followed one hop at a time with the cookies and referrer of each hop only, never down to plain HTTP under Tor or HTTPS-only; a file the server names as an Android app asks before it is saved; connections are dropped the moment the Tor route changes.
Privacy: private tabs' thumbnails and every sheet inherit the screenshot block; the app lock closes open sheets; blank-canvas fingerprinting is defeated; the public suffix list decides what counts as a third-party host; cookies, HTTP auth and the private profile are cleared on a cold start even when the app was killed first.
Blocking: a trailing dot on a host no longer slips past rules; longer addresses are matched in full.
