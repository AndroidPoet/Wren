Android TV: Wren now runs on a television. The remote's D-pad moves a cursor over the page (a tap is a step, holding accelerates), the centre key presses where it points and a held centre key is a long press, the page scrolls when the cursor reaches an edge, and pushing it off the top of a page moves into the address bar. The chrome is one row at the top; sites get their desktop layout. Appears in the TV launcher.
